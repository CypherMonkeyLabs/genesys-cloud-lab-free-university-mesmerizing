What you practise

Build the path

Publish an Architect-style flow, then route with bullseye rings, preferred agents, and evaluation methods. Drafts do not answer.

Hear the difference

SIP sets the call up. RTP moves the audio. The Edge, BYOC Cloud, and BYOC Premises stay separate objects.

Check the page

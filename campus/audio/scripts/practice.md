# How to practise and share

Two architect voices. Generated offline with espeak-ng. Not a Genesys recording.

- A: Practise on a track. Each lesson has a figure, a step-through, a six-question quiz, flashcards, a lab, and a fault with a fix.
- B: Finish the quiz and the lab in this browser. Progress stays on this device. The lab does not call Genesys Cloud.
- A: When a page says confirm on the vendor page, open the citation. Do not invent a price, an exam code, a customer name, or a login host.
- B: Regions names three login hosts this lab has checked. US East, US West, and FedRAMP. Purple dots on that map are examples, not coverage and not carriers.
- A: Share the lab from the footer. The repository link is where you rate, comment, or ask for help. cyphermonkeylabs.com is the studio. The contact form is the only door. We do not publish an email.
- B: Save for offline from the top bar. The zip on the cheat-code shelf packs the sheets and the flashcards.
